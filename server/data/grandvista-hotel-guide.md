# The GrandVista Hotel — Guest Guide & Policies

## About The GrandVista Hotel

The GrandVista Hotel is a 4-star property located at 88 Harbor View Road, Seaport City. The hotel has 220 rooms across 12 floors, including Standard, Deluxe, Executive Suite, and Presidential Suite categories. The hotel opened in 2011 and underwent a full renovation in 2023.

## Check-in and Check-out

Standard check-in time is 3:00 PM and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Early check-in is subject to availability and may incur a fee of $25. Late check-out until 2:00 PM can be requested at the front desk for a fee of $35, subject to availability; late check-out after 2:00 PM is charged at 50% of the nightly room rate.

Guests must present a valid government-issued photo ID and the credit card used for booking at check-in. The minimum check-in age is 18 years old.

## Room Rates and Booking

Room rates vary by season:
- Low season (January–March, November): Standard room from $120/night
- Mid season (April–June, September–October): Standard room from $160/night
- High season (July–August, December): Standard room from $220/night

Deluxe rooms are priced 30% above Standard rooms. Executive Suites are priced 80% above Standard rooms. Presidential Suites are priced at $650/night flat rate year-round.

A refundable security deposit of $100 is required at check-in, charged to the credit card on file and released within 7 business days after check-out, provided no damages or incidentals are charged.

## Cancellation Policy

Reservations can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before the scheduled check-in date. Cancellations made within 48 hours of check-in are charged one night's room rate. No-shows are charged the full amount of the first night's stay. Group bookings of 6 or more rooms follow a separate policy requiring 14 days' notice for free cancellation.

## Breakfast and Dining

Complimentary breakfast buffet is included with Executive Suite and Presidential Suite bookings, served in the Harbor View Restaurant on the 2nd floor from 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM daily. Standard and Deluxe room guests may purchase breakfast for $18 per person per day.

The hotel has three dining venues:
1. Harbor View Restaurant (breakfast, lunch, dinner) — 2nd floor
2. The Lighthouse Bar (drinks, light snacks) — Lobby level, open 4:00 PM to midnight
3. Sunset Rooftop Grill (dinner only, seasonal, April–October) — 12th floor rooftop, reservations recommended

Room service is available 24 hours a day with a $6 delivery charge per order.

## Amenities

The GrandVista Hotel offers the following amenities:
- Outdoor infinity pool (open 7:00 AM–9:00 PM, heated November–March)
- Fitness center (open 24 hours, keycard access)
- Full-service spa (appointments required, open 9:00 AM–8:00 PM)
- Business center with printing services (open 8:00 AM–6:00 PM)
- Free high-speed Wi-Fi throughout the property
- Valet parking ($30/night) and self-parking ($18/night)
- 30 car parking & 50 2 wheeler parking facility available.
- Electric vehicle charging stations (4 available, first-come first-served)

## Pet Policy

The GrandVista Hotel is pet-friendly in designated pet-friendly rooms only, located on the 3rd and 4th floors. A non-refundable pet fee of $50 per stay applies, covering up to two pets under 50 lbs each. Pets must not be left unattended in rooms at any time. Service animals are welcome throughout the property at no charge and are not subject to the pet-friendly room restriction.

## Smoking Policy

The GrandVista Hotel is a 100% non-smoking property, including all guest rooms, balconies, and indoor public areas. Smoking is permitted only in the designated outdoor smoking area near the parking garage entrance. Guests who smoke in non-designated areas will be charged a $250 cleaning fee.

## Loyalty Program

The GrandVista Rewards program has three tiers: Silver, Gold, and Platinum. Members earn 10 points per dollar spent on room rates. Silver tier (0–5,000 points) offers late check-out priority. Gold tier (5,001–15,000 points) adds complimentary breakfast and room upgrades subject to availability. Platinum tier (15,001+ points) adds free valet parking and a dedicated concierge line. Points expire 24 months after they are earned if the account remains inactive.

## Accessibility

The hotel offers 10 ADA-compliant accessible rooms featuring roll-in showers, grab bars, and lowered fixtures. Accessible rooms are located on the 1st and 2nd floors near elevator access. Wheelchairs are available for loan at the front desk free of charge, subject to availability. Service animals are permitted throughout the hotel.

## Meeting and Event Spaces

The GrandVista Hotel has 5 meeting rooms and 1 grand ballroom. The ballroom accommodates up to 300 guests banquet-style or 450 theater-style. Meeting room rental starts at $500/day for the smallest room (up to 20 people) and goes up to $3,000/day for the ballroom. Audio-visual equipment rental is available separately. A 25% deposit is required to confirm any event booking, due at the time of contract signing.

## Transportation

The hotel offers a complimentary airport shuttle running every 30 minutes between 5:00 AM and 11:00 PM to Seaport International Airport, located 12 miles from the property. Shuttle requests outside these hours can be arranged with the concierge for a $40 fee. Taxi and rideshare pickup is available at the main entrance.

## Contact and Front Desk

The front desk is staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and can be reached by dialing 0 from any in-room phone. The general hotel phone number is (555) 018-2200. Lost and found inquiries should be directed to the front desk; unclaimed items are donated to charity after 90 days.
